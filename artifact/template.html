Every time it changed its mind

    What this run shows about the bot

    • It corrected itself. Each time its rules fell below 50% right, it replaced them, and it ended on rules that were holding up.
    • It also chased luck. Some challengers took over after a hot streak, then slipped back to 40–48%. With 20–60 trades, the best-looking rule set is often just lucky.
    • Right more often is not the same as making money. Some rule sets were right a third of the time yet averaged a profit, because a few winners were large.
